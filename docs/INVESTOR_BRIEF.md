# LegalEase.AI — Investor Brief (Illustrative)

> Financial figures below are **projections for planning**, not audited results.

## Why LegalEase wins

- **India-first legal AI** with BNS/BNSS-aware tooling during code transition
- **Data sovereignty:** KB synthesis on local Ollama; Gemini isolated from private documents
- **Vertical depth:** Matters, CRM 2.0, billing, e-discovery, matter intelligence — not a chat wrapper

## Market

- Indian legal services TAM: large and fragmented; digitization accelerating
- Initial SOM: solo practitioners and 2–20 lawyer firms on Pro / Legal Pro

## Moat

1. Tenant-scoped RAG + confidence gates (trust layer)
2. Matter intelligence pipeline (entities, timeline, contradictions, risk score)
3. Firm workflow embedding (intake → matter → billing → discovery)
4. Data flywheel: feedback learning queue → human review → retrieval tuning
5. Production guards + env audit tooling for enterprise deploy confidence
